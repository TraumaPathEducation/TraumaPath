Rib Fracture

A rib fracture is a break in one or more ribs. Injury can produce significant pain and may affect normal breathing mechanics.

1. Normal Anatomy The rib cage protects the lungs and other structures within the chest.
2. Injury Mechanism Significant force to the chest can exceed the strength of a rib and produce a fracture.
3. What's Happening Inside Pain from the injured rib can make deep breathing difficult and nearby tissues may also be affected.
4. Signs & Symptoms Chest tenderness, pain with breathing or movement, and difficulty taking a deep breath may occur.
