Rib Cage

The rib cage forms a protective framework around the chest and helps support normal breathing mechanics.

Primary Role Protects the lungs, heart, and other structures within the thorax.
Function / Movement The ribs move with breathing as the chest expands and contracts.

Rib Fracture — Overview

A rib fracture is a break in one or more ribs. Pain can limit deep breathing and nearby tissues may also be affected.

TraumaPath connects the injury back to normal anatomy so the learner can understand why symptoms occur.
